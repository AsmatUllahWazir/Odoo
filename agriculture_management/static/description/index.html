Core Features

Everything you need to run a modern, data-driven farm.

🗺️

Field & Parcel Management

Map your farm with GIS coordinates, soil type, pH levels, irrigation systems, and elevation data. Track usable vs total area for precise planning.

  • GeoJSON polygon support for boundary mapping
  • Soil classification (clay, sandy, loamy, etc.)
  • Irrigation type & availability tracking
  • Real-time field status (Fallow, Active, Harvested)
🌱

Crop & Season Planning

Plan every growing season with budget targets, expected yields, and automatic harvest date calculations based on crop growing periods.

  • Season lifecycle: Planning → Active → Harvesting → Closed
  • Budget vs Actual cost tracking
  • Auto-calculated expected harvest dates
  • Yield variance analysis (% above/below target)
🧪

Input Applications

Log every input application with full traceability — fertilizer, pesticide, fungicide, irrigation, and soil amendments linked to stock lots.

  • Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) tracking for compliance
  • Re-entry interval & safety equipment logging
  • Weather conditions at time of application
  • Auto-create stock consumption moves
🚜

Harvest Management

Record harvests with quality grading, moisture content, and automatic inventory reception. Generate unique traceability codes for every batch.

  • Quality grades: Premium, Standard, Economy, Rejected
  • Moisture content & drying date tracking
  • Auto-link to stock lots and warehouse locations
  • Harvest wizard for streamlined data entry
🌦️

Weather Integration

Log daily weather data manually or fetch from external APIs. Correlate weather patterns with crop performance and disease outbreaks.

  • Support for OpenWeatherMap & WeatherAPI
  • Temperature, humidity, precipitation, wind, UV tracking
  • Crop impact assessment (Positive/Neutral/Negative/Severe)
  • Historical weather analysis per field
🐛

Disease & Pest Tracking

Detect, treat, and analyze crop health incidents. Track treatment effectiveness and estimate financial impact of outbreaks.

  • Incident types: Disease, Pest, Weed, Weather, Nutrient
  • Severity levels with visual alerts
  • Photo & document attachments per incident
  • Estimated yield loss & financial impact
⚙️

Equipment Management

Track all farm machinery with operating hours, depreciation, fuel usage, and maintenance schedules linked to Odoo Maintenance module.

  • Auto-calculated book value & depreciation
  • One-click maintenance request creation
  • Current location tracking per field
  • Fuel type & capacity management
📈

Seasonal P&L & Reporting

Automatic profit/loss calculation per season, field, and crop. Generate professional PDF reports for stakeholders and compliance.

  • Real-time revenue, cost, and margin tracking
  • Graph & Pivot views for trend analysis
  • QWeb PDF Season Report
  • Multi-currency support
🔗

Full Traceability

Trace any harvest back through every input application, cultivation record, and field condition. Perfect for organic certification and export compliance.

  • Unique traceability codes per harvest
  • Public portal for customer queries
  • Complete chain: Input → Cultivation → Harvest → Sale
  • Lot-level tracking for recalls
