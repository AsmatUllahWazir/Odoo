💇 Salon & Spa Management

Complete salon management solution with appointment booking, POS integration, membership management, and advanced analytics

⭐ Odoo 18 🏆 App Store Approved 🔒 Secure

📋 Overview

Salon & Spa Management is a comprehensive Odoo module designed specifically for salons, spas, and beauty centers. It streamlines operations from appointment booking to staff management, inventory, and financial reporting.

✨ Key Benefits

  • 📈 Increase revenue with automated appointment management
  • 👥 Improve customer experience with online booking and reminders
  • 💰 Optimize staff utilization with intelligent scheduling
  • 📊 Make data-driven decisions with detailed analytics
  • 🔄 Save time with automated workflows and notifications

🎯 Core Features

📅 Appointment Management

Complete appointment lifecycle from booking to completion with calendar view, drag-and-drop scheduling, and automated notifications.

👩‍💼 Staff Management

Manage staff skills, schedules, commissions, and performance with detailed reporting and analytics.

💺 Chair Management

Track chair availability, assignments, and revenue with interactive dashboard and visual status indicators.

🏆 Membership & Loyalty

Create tiered membership plans with discounts, track member benefits, and build customer loyalty.

🛒 POS Integration

Seamless integration with Odoo POS for check-in, check-out, billing, and payment processing.

📊 Analytics & Reporting

Comprehensive dashboards and reports for revenue, staff performance, and business growth tracking.

👥 User Roles & Access

🏢 Salon Manager

Full access to all features including configuration, reporting, and staff management. Can create services, manage memberships, and view all analytics.

💇 Salon Staff

Access to appointments, check-in/out, view assigned services, and track personal commissions. Limited to own appointments and performance data.

👤 Customers

Online portal access for booking, viewing appointments, managing memberships, and accessing loyalty benefits.

📦 Installation

  1. Download the module from Odoo App Store
  2. Place the module in your Odoo addons directory
  3. Update the apps list (Apps → Update Apps List)
  4. Search for "Salon & Spa Management"
  5. Click Install
  6. Configure your services, staff, and chairs
  7. Start managing your salon!
