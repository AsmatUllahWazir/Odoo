Detailed Operational Workflows & System Architecture

📋 Appointment Booking Workflow

  • Online Booking: Customer selects service, staff, and time slot.
  • Availability Check: Real-time staff and chair availability.
  • Confirmation: Appointment confirmed with email notification.
  • Check-in: Customer arrives and checks in at POS.
  • Service: Staff performs service with status tracking.
  • Completion: Appointment marked complete with order generation.

💳 POS & Billing Workflow

  • Order Creation: POS creates order from appointment.
  • Service Selection: Add or modify services at checkout.
  • Member Discount: Automatic discounts for members.
  • Payment Processing: Multiple payment methods supported.
  • Invoice Generation: Professional invoice with service details.
  • Commission Calculation: Automatic staff commission tracking.

🏆 Membership & Loyalty Workflow

  • Plan Selection: Customer chooses membership tier.
  • Card Generation: Barcode card created with unique number.
  • Discount Application: Automatic discounts on all services.
  • Points Accumulation: Loyalty points earned per service.
  • Point Redemption: Redeem points for free services.
  • Auto-Renewal: Automated renewal with email reminders.

📊 Analytics & Monitoring

  • Real-time Dashboards: Appointments, revenue, staff activity.
  • Revenue Analysis: By service, staff, chair, and time period.
  • Staff Performance: Commission, service volume, and satisfaction.
  • Chair Utilization: Occupancy rates and revenue per chair.
  • Customer Analytics: Visit frequency, spending patterns, and retention.

Enterprise Backend Configuration & Access Rights

Granular Security Groups Matrix

The module defines 3 distinct security access groups inside Odoo 18 User Settings. Each role strictly controls what the logged-in user can view, create, edit, or export:

Security Role Intended Staff Role Permissions & Capabilities
Salon Staff Stylists / Therapists Manage own appointments, check-in/out, view commission, and track personal performance.
Salon Supervisor Senior Stylists / Supervisors All staff permissions plus staff scheduling, service configuration, and chair management.
Salon Manager Salon Owners / Administrators Complete administrative control over all salon data, configuration, reporting, and user management.

Automated Workflows & Business Logic

  • Appointment Workflow: Draft → Confirmed → Checked In → In Progress → Completed.
  • Order Workflow: Draft → Confirmed → In Progress → Done → Cancelled.
  • Commission Calculation: Automatic commission calculation based on fixed or section rules.
  • Membership Auto-Renewal: Automated renewal with expiry notifications.
  • Email Notifications: Automated confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups.
  • Inventory Deduction: Automatic stock deduction when product kits are used.
  • Revenue Tracking: Real-time revenue tracking by service, staff, and chair.
