Saudi HR Government Compliance Hub

A structured Saudi workforce compliance and Muqeem-oriented operations layer for Odoo 17. Connect employee identity, Iqama and document expiry, government service requests, travel readiness, approvals, payments, transactions, charges, audit and reporting.

Detailed workflows

1. Workforce profile lifecycle

HR links an employee, captures government data, evaluates risk, refreshes calculated indicators, queues required operations and monitors open requests.

Link employee→Capture data→Evaluate risk→Refresh state→Queue operation→Monitor

Calculated indicators include days to expiry, weighted risk score, risk level, operational state, travel readiness, open requests, failed transactions and outstanding charges.

2. Government service request lifecycle

A request moves through controlled business gates:

Draft→Pre-check→Documents→Approval→Payment→Ready→Submitted→Government→Approved→Done

The request contains service type, employee, company, eligibility score, blockers, checklist, documents, charges, approvals, SLA information and linked transactions.

Exception states: query, rejected and cancelled. These preserve interruption reasons and prevent incomplete work from appearing as completed.

3. Transaction lifecycle

Queued→Submitted→Processing→Success

Failed and cancelled are also supported. Each transaction records provider mode, request/response payloads, external reference, attempts, duration and retry information.

4. Travel readiness lifecycle

Plan travel→Validate documents→Score readiness→Monitor return

Readiness is classified as green, amber or red according to permit dates, return deadline, passport validity, Iqama validity and current travel status.

5. Sponsorship transfer

Draft→Eligibility→Approval→Payment→Submitted→New sponsor acceptance→Processing→Done

6. Occupation change

Draft→Eligibility→Approval→Government→Done

Security and integration boundary

Access levels

  • User: assigned operational records and follow-up.
  • Manager: broader workforce, approval, risk and charge visibility.
  • Administrator: configuration, transactions, rules and audit.
  • Company-level record protection and role-based access.

Integration requirements

The module is provider-neutral. A live Muqeem connection requires an approved provider/API arrangement, credentials, exact endpoints, payload specifications, authentication/signing, encryption, idempotency, rate limits, response mapping, error handling and production security review.

Summary: The module connects employee government identity with expiry risk, service requests, validation gates, approvals, payments, transactions, travel readiness, charges, portal access, audit and reporting. It is intended to make Saudi HR government operations measurable, traceable and controlled.
