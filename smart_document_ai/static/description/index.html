Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. If you don't find what you're looking for, feel free to contact our support team.

What versions of Odoo are supported?

Smart Document AI is fully compatible with Odoo 17 Community and Enterprise editions. We also offer backward compatibility for Odoo 16 upon request for Enterprise customers. The module is tested and certified for seamless integration.

Is this a one-time purchase or subscription?

Smart Document AI is a one-time purchase with lifetime updates included. There are no recurring fees, no hidden costs, and no subscription traps. Pay once and enjoy the module forever with all future updates at no additional charge.

How does the AI categorization work?

Our AI engine analyzes document content, metadata, and structure to automatically suggest categories. It uses machine learning algorithms that improve over time as you confirm or adjust suggestions. The system supports multiple languages and can be trained on your organization's specific document types for higher accuracy.

What file formats are supported?

Smart Document AI supports all major document formats including PDF, Microsoft Word (DOC/DOCX), Excel (XLS/XLSX), PowerPoint (PPT/PPTX), OpenDocument formats (ODT, ODS, ODP), images (JPG, PNG, TIFF, BMP), and plain text files. OCR functionality enables text extraction from scanned documents and images.

Can I get a demo or trial version?

Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial with full access to all Professional features. No credit card required. Simply install the trial module from the Odoo Apps Store or contact us for a guided demo session with our product specialists.

What kind of support do you offer?

All plans include email support with 24-hour response time. Professional plans include priority support with 4-hour response time. Enterprise customers receive dedicated support with a named account manager, phone support, and guaranteed SLA. We also provide comprehensive documentation and video tutorials.
