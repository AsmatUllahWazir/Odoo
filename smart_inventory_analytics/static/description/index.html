Dead Stock Detection & Scoring

RISK ENGINE

Products with quantity on hand that have not moved beyond a configurable threshold are flagged as dead stock. A composite score prioritises the worst offenders by days, quantity and value.

  • Configurable threshold — default 90 days (Inventory → Settings)
  • Dead stock score — days × quantity factor × value factor
  • List & form views — badges, filters and group-by on products
  • Capital visibility — total and dead stock value on dashboard and reports
🛒

Turnover & Smart Reorder Intelligence

PROCUREMENT

30-day sold quantity and turnover rate per product. Suggested reorder qty, priority (None → Critical) and a clear reason — so planners know what to act on first.

  • Priorities: None, Low, Medium, High, Critical
  • Reasons: Out of stock, below minimum, high velocity, dead stock review, etc.
  • Uses reordering min/max when configured on the product

How the Module Works

📦 Aging & Dead Stock Flow

  • Stock moves update product last move date.
  • Days since last move and aging bucket are computed and stored.
  • If qty > 0 and days ≥ threshold → marked as dead stock.
  • Score combines days, quantity and cost for prioritisation.
  • Lists, dashboard and reports surface the worst items first.

🛒 Reorder Intelligence Flow

  • 30-day customer deliveries drive sold qty and turnover.
  • Compared to reordering min/max when set on the product.
  • Suggested qty and priority (critical → none) are computed.
  • Reason text explains why (out of stock, below min, velocity…).
  • High/critical items appear on dashboard and in PDF filters.

📷 Analysis Snapshot Flow

  • Create an Aging Analysis in draft.
  • Click Run Analysis to load all products with stock.
  • Lines store bucket, dead flag, score, turnover, reorder data.
  • Totals and bucket distribution are stored on the analysis.
  • Optional daily cron can create analyses automatically.

📄 Report & Portal Flow

  • Open Print Aging Report wizard and set filters.
  • Generate PDF with summary KPIs and full product table.
  • Portal users open /my/inventory/aging from My Account.
  • Filter by dead stock or high reorder; sort by score, days, value.

Settings & Automation

  • Dead stock threshold (days) — Inventory → Configuration → Settings
  • Daily automatic analysis — optional; activate the related scheduled action
  • Multi-company — analyses and reports respect company
  • Extends product.product, stock.quant — no parallel product master

  • Sort by score, days since move, stock value or name
  • Paginated list of products with qty, value, bucket, score, priority
  • Only users in Smart Inventory groups see the entry
